2023: The best way individuals believe to achieve happiness (or: The best way to obtain a sense of happiness in 2023)

Personally, I think the best way to achieve happiness is to transform life from a multiplayer online game into a single-player game. Online games like PUBG (Chicken Dinner) or Honor of Kings (King’s Land/Arena of Valor) require you to be better than others, have superior gear, and even compare skins. There are always stronger players among the strong, so disappointment is a highly probable event. But what if we change life into a single-player game? For example, Zelda—all you need to do is compare yourself to yourself. Every step is filled with a sense of happiness. Projecting this onto reality means having a better physique than others, a bigger house than others, and a fancier car than others. Constantly thinking about being superior to others, about having to surpass them, makes happiness difficult to achieve. In my opinion, the correct method is: buying a house so you don’t have to move around all the time, happy. Buying a car so you don’t have to squeeze onto the subway, happy. 浪费时间是最大的痛苦

Time-Related Pains Most productivity-related issues cause temporal pain: people feel like they’re wasting time. These pains are caused by suboptimal processes and friction between tasks. If tedious work takes a lot of time, it keeps you from doing important and useful things instead. That leads to time mismanagement and relevant actions not being taken. By solving the time-related problem, productive tasks can be accomplished faster and sooner. When people complain about inefficiencies, tedium, or pointless work, you’re looking at a time-related problem.

What should I do when the boss always assigns me miscellaneous tasks/grunt work?

First, categorize things, and ensure that the core KPIs consume at least 60% or more of your time and effort. If other miscellaneous tasks take up more than this, then communicate with your manager/leader. Don’t complain that you have too much work; instead, tell your manager that you are concerned about the completion rate and quality of your core KPIs being affected. Usually, your manager gives you temporary miscellaneous tasks without fully considering your workload; they might just assign them arbitrarily, or they might think that giving them to you is fine—don’t let it slide.
