2024-10-08#

  • A-shares started trading today, and the entire market hit the daily limit up immediately upon opening. I cannot judge whether it’s retail investor madness or manipulation by an invisible hand. Intuitively, retail investors shouldn’t just place buy orders at the limit-up price. But the trading volume is also very high, which confuses me. High volume means someone is selling at the limit-up price, and someone else is taking it. If it’s not just me playing with myself and losing transaction fees, then a large number of retail investors are truly entering the market.
  • The limit-up board wasn’t sealed; it quickly slid downwards, ultimately rising 4.59% with a turnover of 3.5 trillion.
  • Companies started announcing share reductions last week. When market sentiment is so high, some are reducing holdings even when they are making money, which must have underlying reasons. I think the main reason is that they themselves don’t believe in their own companies.
  • I can’t figure out who is taking the position with real money.

2024-10-10#

  • Today is a special day, worth remembering.
  • Operated my account this afternoon, selling 50% of Industrial and Commercial Bank of China shares.
  • The small-cap market fell overall today, while large-cap blue chips rose 4-5%. At the same time, the Ministry of Finance announced a press conference on Saturday. After analyzing, I believe that policymakers want to temporarily stabilize the market by pushing up large-cap stocks and releasing news to stabilize market confidence. The press conference on Saturday will definitely introduce some practical policies to pull the market back, because their goals are far from achieved, and they still have many moves left. They cannot give up on the market now.
  • Based on the above judgment, I decided to sell in batches today and tomorrow, selling 50% of the current holdings each time, and gradually repurchasing them on Monday and Tuesday next week.
  • This operation violates my most important principle: Don’t try to TIMING the market. But I also want to test with real money whether my recent understanding is correct, and it cannot be denied that I also have some greedy desire to make money.

2024-10-17#

  • The judgment on the 10th didn’t fully consider the factor of unfinished projects.
  • We may need to continuously correct our judgments because the world is changing.
  • So, do we also need to be flexible and constantly “flip the big cake” (i.e., adjust/change strategy)?
  • Maintain Flexibility: Because the world changes rapidly, we need to constantly correct our judgments and strategies. Whether as investors, policymakers, or individuals, we should adjust based on new information and changing environments to avoid falling into difficulties due to outdated judgments.
  • Dynamic Response: Whether it’s economic policy or investment decisions, a dynamic response strategy should be adopted. Be vigilant about the effectiveness of economic stimulus measures, observe their feedback on the real economy, and adjust promptly according to new circumstances. Maintain a balanced perspective on long-term trends and short-term fluctuations, avoiding over-reliance on a certain measure or theory.
  • The way to conduct oneself in the world is also the art of adapting; how can one be stubbornly biased in one direction?
