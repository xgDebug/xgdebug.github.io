Big Brother's Control in Nineteen Eighty-Four

December 29, 2024 · 3 min · xgDebug | Translations:
  • 中文

The mental and physical means used by Big Brother to achieve eternal power:

Mental Means:

These means are designed to deeply control the people’s thoughts, emotions, and cognition, dismantling their independence and any form of social connection.

  1. Radical Control of Information and Historical Revisionism:

    • Systematic Rewriting, Falsification, and Destruction of Historical Records: Completely erasing objective facts, shaping a past that perfectly suits the needs of Ingsoc, causing the people to lose reference points and be unable to question the present.
    • Monopoly of All Information Sources and Information Blockade: Ingsoc controls all media, education, and cultural channels, isolating external information to ensure the people only receive the content that Ingsoc wishes them to receive.
    • Creation and Dissemination of Massive Amounts of False Information and Propaganda: Spreading lies and distorting facts through various channels, confusing public opinion, making it difficult for the people to distinguish truth from falsehood.

  2. Deep-seated Thought Control and Psychological Manipulation:

    • Forced Implementation of “Doublethink”: Compelling the people to simultaneously accept and believe two contradictory beliefs, destroying logic and rationality, and causing them to lose the ability to think independently.
    • Creation and Popularization of “Newspeak”: Fundamentally limiting the people’s cognitive abilities by artificially restricting the vocabulary, especially words that express complex emotions, abstract concepts, and independent thought.
    • Ubiquitous Propaganda and Slogan Inculcation: Constantly indoctrinating the people with Ingsoc’s ideology through omnipresent slogans, mottos, and posters, creating enemies and stimulating fanaticism and hatred.
    • Using “Two Minutes Hate” for Emotional Manipulation: A mandatory daily activity that directs the people’s negative emotions toward designated “enemies,” diverting potential dissatisfaction with Ingsoc, and strengthening collective identity.
    • Systematic Stripping of Individuality and Forced Cultivation of Collective Identity: Emphasizing collectivism above all else, devaluing individual worth, and erasing personality through uniform clothing, behavioral norms, collective activities, and ideological indoctrination, making the individual a tool of Ingsoc.
    • Constant Generation of Fear and Anxiety: Creating a continuous atmosphere of fear by promoting external threats and internal “enemies,” causing the people to cling to Ingsoc in search of security.
    • Elaborate Cult of Personality Around “Big Brother”: Deifying Big Brother, shaping him into an omniscient and omnipotent figure, making him the object of the people’s blind worship and loyalty.
    • Distortion and Control of Emotions, Especially Loyalty and Love: Suppressing personal emotions, especially love and familial affection, and encouraging absolute loyalty and unconditional obedience to Ingsoc, placing Ingsoc above everything else.

  3. Destroying Family Trust and Creating Atomized Individuals:

    • Using Children for Ideological Indoctrination and Surveillance, Establishing Organizations Similar to “Young Pioneers”: Encouraging children to be absolutely loyal to Ingsoc from a young age, and having them monitor or even denounce their own parents. This completely destroys trust among family members, turning the family from a haven of emotional support into a place of mutual suspicion, ultimately atomizing society and leaving the individual completely isolated, dependent only on Ingsoc. Children are cultivated as Ingsoc’s “little spies,” becoming tools to maintain Ingsoc’s rule.
    • Encouraging Denunciation and Mutual Surveillance: Exploiting human weakness, encouraging neighbors, colleagues, and even spouses to denounce one another, further eroding social trust, causing everyone to live in fear and insecurity.

  4. Comprehensive Control of Education and Culture:

    • The Education System Serves Entirely the Party’s Ideology: School curricula are thoroughly reformed, subjecting children to brainwashing education from a young age, cultivating their blind obedience to Ingsoc and their instinctive hatred of dissent.
    • Strict Censorship and Control of Culture and Arts: Any form of cultural or artistic work is strictly censored by Ingsoc, ensuring that the content disseminated meets the needs of the Party and stifles independent thought and creativity.

Physical Means:

These means directly affect the people’s bodies, behavior, and living environment, ensuring absolute obedience through coercion and violent intimidation.

  1. Ubiquitous and All-Encompassing Surveillance and Monitoring:

    • Widespread Deployment and Mandatory Use of Telescreens: Two-way monitoring devices deployed in every corner, placing the people under the surveillance of Ingsoc at all times and in all places, even within their own homes, leaving them with no privacy.
    • Secret Activities and Arbitrary Arrests by the Thought Police: The secret police are responsible for monitoring and arresting anyone suspected of having a “thought crime,” creating an omnipresent sense of fear.
    • Encouraging Denunciation and Creating a Social Climate of High Pressure: Utilizing fear and reward mechanisms, encouraging the people to denounce one another, creating a social atmosphere where everyone is constantly worried about themselves.

  2. Brutal Violent Suppression and a Severe Punishment Mechanism:

    • Arbitrary Arrests, Imprisonment, and Cruel Physical and Mental Torture: Anyone deemed to threaten Ingsoc’s rule faces arrest, imprisonment, and inhumane torture, aimed at destroying their will.
    • “Vaporization” - Complete Erasure of Individual Existence: Not only is the “criminal” physically eliminated, but they are also completely deleted from all social records, making it seem as if they never existed, serving as a deterrent to others.
    • Intimidation Effect of Public Trials and Parades: Utilizing public trials and parades to intimidate the people, demonstrating the miserable fate of those who disobey Ingsoc, and reinforcing Ingsoc’s absolute authority.

  3. Controlling Basic Living Resources and Maintaining Survival Pressure:

    • Implementation of a Strict Rationing System: Controlling the supply of basic necessities such as food and clothing, forcing the people to rely on Ingsoc for survival, thereby reducing their willingness to resist.
    • Artificially Creating Scarcity of Resources and Hard Living Conditions: Deliberately creating resource shortages, causing the people to rush around desperately for survival, leaving them no time to worry about politics, and focusing their energy on survival rather than challenging authority.

  4. Strict Control of Social Structure and Institutional Constraints:

    • Implementation of a Rigid Social Hierarchy: Dividing society into different classes, such as Inner Party, Outer Party, and Proles, limiting social mobility and ensuring that power remains in the hands of a select few.
    • Comprehensive Control of Work and Occupational Allocation: Ingsoc arranges everyone’s work, ensuring it completely serves the needs of the Party, limiting personal development and free choice.
    • Strict Intervention in Marriage, Reproduction, and Family Life: Intervening in personal marriage and reproduction, encouraging procreation for the benefit of Ingsoc, but suppressing personal emotions to prevent individual loyalty from being dispersed.
    • Mandatory Participation in Various Collective Activities and Political Rituals: Such as morning exercises, political rallies, and parades, aimed at cultivating discipline, obedience, and collective consciousness, while also serving as a public display of loyalty.

Summary:

To achieve the goal of eternal power, Big Brother and Ingsoc employ an extremely precise and brutal combination of tactics. The mental and physical means work together and reinforce one another. The mental means aim to fundamentally destroy the people’s independent consciousness and will to resist, making them internally accept and obey Ingsoc’s rule; the physical means, through ubiquitous surveillance, violent suppression, and control over basic living resources, ensure that the people’s actions completely align with the will of Ingsoc.
