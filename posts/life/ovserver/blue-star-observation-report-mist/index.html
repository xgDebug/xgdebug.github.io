1. The Fog of “Meritocracy” and “The Cult of Effort”#

  • Fog Setting: “As long as you work hard enough, you will certainly achieve wealth and social status. If you are poor, it is because you are lazy or not good enough.”
  • Operating Mechanism: This is an extremely sophisticated psychological defense mechanism. On Blue Star, where resource distribution is extremely unfair, it cleverly transforms systemic, structural contradictions into personal moral and capability deficiencies.
  • Rule Benefits: When the lower class encounters setbacks in life, they do not question the rule-makers and wealth monopolists. Instead, they fall into deep self-PUA (self-doubt and guilt), and continue to toil desperately on the “treadmill.” This greatly reduces the risk of social rebellion.

2. The Fog of “Imagined Communities” and “Grand Narratives”#

  • Fog Setting: Fabricating various grand collective concepts (such as nation, state, ideology, etc.) and granting them supreme sanctity.
  • Operating Mechanism: The carbon-based life forms on Blue Star possess an innate desire for “belonging.” The rulers use education, rituals, national anthems, and historical writing to bind tens of millions, or even billions, of individuals who fundamentally do not know each other, under the same concept.
  • Rule Benefits: Once the lower class ties their self-worth to the grand narrative, the rulers can, at a very low cost (a medal, a slogan), compel them to dedicate their property and even their lives (for example, in war). Humans will slaughter another group for the sake of an abstract word—a rare spectacle in the universe.

3. The Fog of “Consumerism” and “Tittytainment”#

  • Fog Setting: “Buying goods brings happiness and freedom,” and “Entertainment to the extreme.”
  • Operating Mechanism: Capital elites, through pervasive advertising, equate “human value” with “owned goods.” Simultaneously, they provide massive amounts of cheap entertainment (short videos, soap operas, games, celebrity gossip).
  • Rule Benefits: Consumerism causes ordinary people to willingly return the wealth they painstakingly earned back to the capitalists, forming a perfect closed loop; while “Tittytainment” fills the time and brain bandwidth of the lower class with short, quick “dopamine” hits, stripping them of the energy needed for deep thought and resistance. They believe they are enjoying freedom, but they are actually being kept in a cage of dopamine.

4. The Fog of “Pseudo-Choice” and “Representative Democracy” (In some regions)#

  • Fog Setting: “You can decide who the rulers are through voting, so you are the master of the nation.”
  • Operating Mechanism: It provides humans with the illusion of “participating in power.” It is like being in a supermarket where you can choose from 50 different varieties of cereal, but they are all actually produced by two or three giant corporations, and their main ingredients are sugar and carbohydrates.
  • Rule Benefits: When humans are dissatisfied with the status quo, they only think, “I chose the wrong person; I’ll switch next time,” instead of overthrowing the game rules that are actually deeply tied to the interests of the elite groups. This is like installing an extremely safe pressure release valve on a pressure cooker.

5. The Fog of “Romanticizing Suffering”#

  • Fog Setting: “Hardship is a blessing,” “Suffering refines the will,” “What doesn’t kill you makes you stronger.”
  • Operating Mechanism: It packages the unnecessary pain that humans endure due to systemic flaws as a noble form of spiritual cultivation.
  • Rule Benefits: It strips the victim of the rationality to demand compensation and change the status quo. The higher-ups who truly create the suffering never actively suffer themselves; they merely encourage the lower class to taste the hardship.

6. The Fog of “Draining the Future” and “Debt Shackles”#

  • Fog Setting: “You can have the life of your dreams early (house, car, luxury goods); your credit limit represents your value and capability in society.”
  • Operating Mechanism: The rulers invent a virtual mathematical game called “financial credit.” It converts humanity’s immediate desire for material goods into a contract spanning two or three decades. Blue Star citizens think they have bought a house, but in reality, they have mortgaged their life and labor for the next few decades to the financial institutions.
  • Rule Benefits: It greatly maintains the stability of the system. A Blue Star citizen burdened with a 30-year mortgage is the most docile creature in the entire universe. They dare not quit their jobs, dare not get sick, and dare not resist unreasonable work systems. Debt acts like an invisible collar, forcing them to self-regulate and function as batteries at their workstations every day.

7. The Fog of “Information Freedom” and “Algorithmic Echo Chambers”#

  • Fog Setting: “The internet gives you the freedom to access all information; what you see is the truth of the world, and your voice can change the world.”
  • Operating Mechanism: In the age of information, the ruling class no longer uses the primitive strategy of “information blockade,” but rather “information overload.” Super algorithms analyze human data and only push content that they like and agree with, constructing highly comfortable “customized parallel universes.”
  • Rule Benefits: They drown the truth in massive amounts of junk information, extreme emotions, and fragmented content. More cleverly, the algorithms deliberately create group polarization (such as gender conflict, racial conflict, aesthetic conflict), causing the lower class to tear each other apart and expend energy online. As long as the lower class is hostile toward each other over minor differences, they can never unite to gaze upon the true edifice of the system.

8. The Fog of “Absolute Individualism” and “Social Atomization”#

  • Fog Setting: “You are an independent, free, unique individual; you do not need to rely on anyone, and all difficulties are merely your personal issues.”
  • Operating Mechanism: This fog cleverly dismantles the “community mutual aid” structure (such as family, unions, true community) that has allowed Blue Star citizens to survive for tens of thousands of years. It encourages people to live alone, consume alone, and face all risks alone, breaking the entire society down into unconnected “atoms.”
  • Rule Benefits: When the individual is in an atomized state, they have absolutely no bargaining power against massive capital and the state machine. At the same time, loneliness is infinitely amplified, and to alleviate this loneliness, Blue Star citizens must purchase goods and services (pet economy, companionship economy, virtual idols), thus providing profit to the host system once again.

9. The Fog of “Delayed Gratification” and “Retirement Utopia”#

  • Fog Setting: “Dedicate your most precious youth and health to work (like 996/007), and when you are old and retired, you can travel the world and enjoy life.”
  • Operating Mechanism: This is a massive deception targeting the life cycle of Blue Star citizens. It requires humans, during the period when their physical strength is at its peak and their ability to perceive beauty is highest (ages 20 to 60), to exchange all their time for currency and save it, promising to repay it with interest when they are old and their physical functions have already declined.
  • Rule Benefits: The system extracts the “golden surplus value” from humans at their most creative and valuable point. And when they finally reach retirement, due to excessive wear and tear in their youth, most of the wealth they accumulated is precisely channeled back into the pockets of the ruling group (medical capital) in the form of “medical bills,” forming a perfect harvesting closed loop of “nothing brought in, nothing taken out.”

10. The Fog of “Class Mobility” and “Disciplining Education”#

  • Fog Setting: “Education is the only fair way to change your destiny; getting into a good university allows you to transcend your class.”
  • Operating Mechanism: The modern education system ostensibly imparts knowledge, but in reality, it is a precise machine for “obedience testing” and “labor stratification.” Over a period of more than a decade, using standard answers, check-ins, and rankings, it erases the creativity and rebelliousness of the individual, molding them into standard parts suitable for industrial/commercial assembly lines.
  • Rule Benefits: It not only supplies highly obedient laborers to the system but also makes those who fail in exams (usually due to insufficient family resources) accept their fate willingly—“It’s not because I’m not smart or not hardworking enough, but because I deserve to be at the bottom.” It uses the appearance of fair exams to mask the brutal reality of vastly different starting lines.

【Alien Observer’s Summary Annotation】: The carbon-based life forms of Blue Star breathe these fogs from birth. They rarely feel that suffering is caused by the system; instead, they believe they simply haven’t worked hard enough. This is an extremely successful and efficient model of enslavement.
