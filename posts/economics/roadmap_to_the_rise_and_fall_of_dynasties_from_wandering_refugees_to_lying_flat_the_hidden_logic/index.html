Roadmap to the Rise and Fall of Dynasties: From “Wandering Refugees” to “Lying Flat” – The Hidden Logic#

History keeps repeating itself, but it’s not just a straight copy. Every powerful dynasty, at its peak, seems unbeatable, with endless resources and total control. But in the end, they all head toward decline and collapse. Is there a common inner logic behind this, something that doesn’t depend on what the rulers want?

By digging deep into how a dynasty works, we can sketch out a clear roadmap. It not only explains the past but also gives us a way to look at and judge what’s happening right now. This process can be broken down into six stages.

Stage 1: Starting the Engine – The “Squeezing Mode” of High Savings and Low Spending#

In the early days of building a dynasty, the main job is to gather resources to support a huge ruling machine (like bureaucrats and the army). To do this, an efficient “squeezing mode” gets set up:

  • Core Setup: Through a bunch of rules (like high housing costs, low benefits, and turning education and healthcare into big industries), they do everything to hold back people’s spending power, while forcing them to work super hard just to survive.
  • Goal: Take the huge value people create through their work and shift it away from everyday spending, turning it into resources the rulers can control (like taxes or foreign cash).
  • Metaphor: It’s like a farmer who wants his horses to eat the least hay possible but run the farthest and pull the heaviest loads. People’s hard work is the “hay” that feeds the whole ruling machine.

In this stage, society looks like it’s booming in a weird way: Big economic numbers look great, infrastructure gets built super fast, but regular people’s lives are full of stress, and they’re not happy at all.

Stage 2: The Machine Grows Bigger – The Tax-Eaters’ Self-Reproduction#

Once the ruling machine is built, it has this built-in urge to keep expanding that you can’t stop.

  • Active Growth: The people who already benefit (the “tax-eaters” class) keep putting their relatives into jobs and creating new positions to hold onto their power and pass it to their families. This pulls more and more people into depending on the system.
  • Passive Growth: As the squeezing gets harder, social problems get sharper. To keep things stable, the machine has to hire more people to control everything (like adding more neighborhood watchers, security checks, or censors). This makes the machine even more expensive to run.

The result? The ruling machine gets huge, eating up more and more “hay.” This forces the rulers to squeeze the bottom even harder, creating a deadly loop.

Stage 3: Shaking the Foundation – From “Refugees Everywhere” to “Lying Flat and Not Having Kids”#

When the squeezing pressure goes beyond what people can handle, pushback starts. But the way people push back changes with the times.

  • Old-School Way: Physical Escape. Back in ancient times, when farmers got crushed by heavy taxes and fees, they’d give up their land and become “wandering refugees.” When enough of them gathered, they’d band together and become the armed force that topples the dynasty. This is an active, fierce physical rebellion.
  • Modern Way: Pulling Out of Society. In today’s world, with intense surveillance and stability controls, active physical fights are super hard. So pushback turns into a passive, quiet social and biological form: “lying flat” (giving up on hustling), “rotting away” (just slacking off), “not getting married,” or “not having kids.”

These two ways are basically the same at heart: They’re the smartest choice a person makes when life gets too tough – to quit the “game.” Refugees quit the production system, and “lying flat and not having kids” breaks down the system’s most basic resource – people power. When the horses realize that no matter how hard they run, they get no reward and might even die faster, they stop, or even refuse to have foals.

Stage 4: The Rulers’ Rescue Efforts – The “Patch-Up Artist’s” Fake Reforms#

When “lying flat” and “not having kids” become normal, a full-blown crisis shows up. At this point, the dynasty doesn’t just sit there – it rolls out a bunch of reforms that look deep to save itself. But since they can’t touch the core perks of the tax-eaters class, these reforms are doomed to be just “patch-up jobs” from the start – not fixing the real sickness, but just slapping makeup on a wall that’s about to fall over. These rescue moves usually focus on three areas:

1. Covering Up the Structure: Creating an Illusion of “Boom Times”#

With the economy slowing and society losing energy, the rulers’ first job is to keep the numbers looking “stable” and “getting better,” to hold onto hopes and stop panic from spreading.

  • Tweaking the Stats: The easiest trick is changing how they count things. By messing with unemployment numbers, growth models, or picking what data to share, they can “create” an economy that’s still growing on paper. But this fake “data boom” clashes so hard with what people feel in real life that it ends up hurting the government’s trust.
  • Printing Money to Pump Things Up: When cash is tight, flooding the system with extra money to boost the economy or pay debts feels like a must. Short-term, it might fake a boom in things like stocks or housing prices, but the cost is long-term inflation that waters down everyone’s wealth – especially the little savings of regular folks – and makes inequality worse.
  • Cranking Up Positive Spin: Turn on all the propaganda tools to spotlight a few success stories and bright futures. Paint the “costs” as “hard work” and “crises” as “chances.” By making an “everything’s great here” vibe in the media, they try to numb people’s pain and keep the surface calm.

2. Redistributing Perks: “Reforms” That Just Shuffle the Deck#

Without messing with the whole tax-eaters’ base, the rulers reshuffle perks inside the group. This makes reforms look bold, but really it’s just moving goodies from old big shots to new ones.

  • Selective Crackdowns: Under the banner of “fighting corruption” or “getting rid of bad guys,” they take down unloyal or too-powerful groups. This pulls power back in and grabs their huge stolen cash for the state (or the rulers’ pocket), easing money woes for a bit. “Taking down big tigers” wins big cheers at first, but people soon see old crooks replaced by new ones, and the squeezing doesn’t stop.
  • “Reviving State Companies” and Upgrading Industries: Wave flags like “tech innovation,” “industry upgrades,” or “building new production power,” and pour tons of national cash into picked industries. This opens doors for insiders to grab deals through approvals and fund handouts, letting loyal groups pile up more wealth. In the end, it turns into a feast for a few, without really boosting the whole economy’s energy.

3. Soothing Minds: Rewriting the Story of “Legitimacy”#

When growth promises can’t hold up, rulers need new mental anchors to keep people loyal and united.

  • Waving the “Fairness” Flag: For growing rich-poor gaps, roll out slogans like “shared prosperity” and give symbolic slaps to wild capitalists. This calms the bottom’s anger, shifting blame from rulers vs. ruled to “people” vs. “a few bad rich guys.” But with the sharing system unchanged, these words end up as hot air.
  • Stressing “Safety” and “Staying Inside”: When outside gets rough, focus inward on “self-reliance” and “inner loops.” This turns outside stress into glue for inside unity, firing up national pride to offset economic and life gripes. It makes people think troubles come from foreign foes, so they must stick with rulers to beat them.
  • Reviving Old Traditions: Push hard for “cultural revival” and “national pride,” digging into ancient stuff for legit reasons and mental strength for today’s rule. Build a big history story to boost belonging and pride, so people blame their bad luck on themselves, not the system’s mess.

But these “patch-ups” have limits. When cold reality keeps popping the bubbles, when reform perks never reach regular folks, and when big slogans can’t fill empty stomachs anymore, the rulers lose all trust. These saves don’t stop the crash – they speed it up by grabbing more power and cash, burning the last bits of social faith, like a useless shot that just hastens the end.

Stage 5: The Rulers’ Dead End – The Power Paradox of Not Being Able to Cut Yourself#

In a crisis, wouldn’t a smart ruler just cut inside spending and ease up on people? Nope, they can’t. That’s the core “power trap” of a top-down system.

  • What Power Really Is: Power isn’t from gods – it’s a deal between the ruler and the machine they rely on (the tax-eaters). The ruler hands out perks (cash, status, special rights), and the class gives back loyalty and follow-through.
  • Hitting One Person vs. the Whole Group: A ruler can easily wipe out one corrupt guy (like raiding a minister’s house) because it’s “scaring the chickens to warn the monkeys,” keeping rules and even winning other officials’ nods. But they absolutely can’t hurt the whole ruling class’s perks (like cutting all officials’ pay).
  • The Deadly “Soft Pushback”: If the ruler tries to cut into their own people, the whole machine goes into “soft resistance”: Policies get delayed, info gets twisted, orders get ignored. The ruler turns into a lonely boss who can’t get anything done.

So in a money crunch, the only choice is to keep the inside happy and dump the pressure times ten onto the outside folks who can’t fight back.

Stage 6: The Last Resort – Failing Force and Broken Stories#

When system stress hits the max and people’s “soft resistance” is everywhere, the dynasty pulls out its final two weapons:

  1. Heavy-Handed Force: Try using laws and violence to ban talk of “lying flat” or “not having kids,” even punish the acts. But this is doomed to flop – you can’t punish “doing nothing,” and watching everyone’s thoughts and lives costs way too much.
  2. Brainwashing Propaganda: Crank the spin machine full blast, hyping “more kids mean more blessings” or “hard work makes riches,” while using big tales like “great nation rising” to numb people. But when the hype clashes huge with real-life pain, it falls apart, trust vanishes, and it becomes a joke.

When even these last tricks fail, it means the ruling machine has used up every way to tweak and control society. It turns into a giant, spinning-empty machine that crashes after burning the last drop of fuel.

How to Use This Roadmap?#

To figure out what stage a dynasty is in, just watch these key signs:

  1. Size of the Machine: Is the tax-eaters group growing or shrinking? What’s the share of people paid by the government?
  2. State of the Base: Are people’s birth rates, marriage rates, and spending moods going up or down? Is “lying flat” and “escaping abroad” a fringe idea or common sense?
  3. Rulers’ Choices: In money squeezes, do policies cut inside costs or ramp up outside squeezes (like more fines or taxes)?
  4. Power of the Story: Do official big tales spark agreement or laughs in everyday talk? How much do people trust the hype?

By tracking these changes, you can guess where this “Dynasty Train” is on the tracks – and how far it is from the end of the line.
