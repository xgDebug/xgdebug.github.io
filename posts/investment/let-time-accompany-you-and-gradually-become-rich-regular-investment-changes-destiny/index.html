3 Are there truly investment assets that only rise and never fall?

Highlight(yellow) - Location 260

Therefore, Warren Buffett always recommends that ordinary people buy the S&P 500 index fund—in China, the corresponding asset for the S&P 500 is the CSI 300 index fund. The advantage of index funds is that they are essentially equivalent to “following the market.” You don’t have to bet on the luck of a single company; instead, you bet on the luck of the entire nation, or the luck of the entire world—in any case, in the long run, the scale of the human economy is constantly growing. Look, as long as it is long-term, it suddenly stops being a gamble, because in the long run, risk can actually be diluted to the point of near elimination.
