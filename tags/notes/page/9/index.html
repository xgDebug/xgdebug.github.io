Law of Blood Compensation

The prerequisite for robbery is that there must be something to steal; the prerequisite for kidnapping and demanding ransom is that the hostage must have the financial capacity to pay the ransom. As the Law of Blood Payment states, the value of a hostage’s life is determined by the willingness and ability of the parties to pay the ransom. Under conditions of equal risk and cost, the richer the hostage, and the wealthier the target of the robbery, the higher the returns from the kidnapping and robbery. Conversely, the poorer the target of the robbery or kidnapping, the lower the returns will be. If the returns are so low that they are not worth the effort, the bandits will cease their activities.

As a dynasty transitions from its middle period toward its final years, it is often accompanied by a series of characteristic changes. The following are some common examples: Political Turmoil and Power Struggles: Over time, the dynasty’s court administration often experiences instability and power struggles. Seize when favorable, and defend when adverse.

This earned him the allegiance of the people. Yet, once he secured the realm and incorporated the entire nation into his cage, he transformed from being generous and compassionate to being harsh and brutal. He raised his whip and butcher’s knife, slaughtering the world, massacring meritorious officials, and becoming a demonic king that made everyone tremble with fear. In truth, it wasn’t Zhu Yuanzhang’s nature that changed, but the circumstances. Before ascending the throne, he sought support from various social classes. After taking power, the common people had become the cattle and sheep under his care, and naturally, he was happy to command them forward with the butcher’s knife and the whip.
