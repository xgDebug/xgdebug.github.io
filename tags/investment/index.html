Financial Independence vs. Dependence If your lifestyle matches or exceeds your income, you are a slave. Long-Term Savings Strategy If financial independence is your goal, your savings rate in these years should be high. On the Stock Market and Investing Fundamental Investment Rules Rule #1: Never lose money. Rule #2: Never forget rule #1. Taking an uncertain world as an investment premise

We should spend our time trying to find value in knowable things like industries, companies, and securities, rather than making decisions based on our predictions about the relatively unknowable general economic world and market performance. Because we cannot accurately know what the future will look like, we must obtain value through methods such as firm belief, opinions derived from analyzing the future, and buying at a lower amount when opportunities arise. We must adopt defensive investments because many outcomes may be unfavorable to us. It is more important to confirm that we can survive in the market under negative outcomes rather than to ensure maximum returns under favorable outcomes**. In order to increase the chance of success, when extreme situations occur, we must emphasize that the actions we take should be contrary to the masses, act proactively when the market is down, and be cautious when the market is high. Because outcomes are inherently highly uncertain, we must view decisions and their consequences—good or bad—with skepticism unless they have been verified by many experiments.

Saving Is for the Poor and Investing Is for the Rich

First, figure out how much you expect to comfortably save in the next year. Next, determine how much you expect your investments to grow in the next year. Finally, compare the two numbers. Which is higher, your expected savings or your expected investment growth? If your expected savings are higher, then you need to focus more on saving money and adding to your investments. However, if your expected investment growth is higher, then spend more time thinking about how to invest what you already have. If the numbers are close to each other, then you should spend time on both.
