Training Dogs and Training Humans - Instrumental Learning

Influencing People’s Behavior Like Training a Dog Shaping is a learning procedure. In this learning procedure, initially, any behavior similar to the target behavior is rewarded; then the standard is gradually raised, and rewards are given only for behaviors that are increasingly similar to the target behavior; finally, rewards are given only when the target behavior actually occurs. Some teachers apply this instrumental behavior shaping method in daily teaching. For example, when training students to think about problems, initially, regardless of what answer the student gives, the teacher gives verbal praise for the student’s willingness to raise their hand; then, rewards are selectively given for those increasingly good answers. ...
