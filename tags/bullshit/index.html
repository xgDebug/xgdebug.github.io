Why do I hate Dialectics so much?

Superficiality, Hindering Deep Analysis: Problem: Simply stating that “everything has two sides” is like applying a label, stopping further thought and exploration. Without digging deeper into what those two sides are, the specific conditions that generate them, and which side is more important in a specific context, how to weigh them. Analysis: This assertion simplifies a complex issue into a simple formula, masking the deeper causes, mechanisms, and influences behind the problem. It easily satisfies people with a superficial understanding, lacking the drive to trace the root cause, ultimately leading to a shallow grasp of the issue and preventing the proposal of effective solutions. For example, when discussing the impact of a policy, if one only says “it has pros and cons,” without analyzing what the specific benefits are, what the drawbacks are, and what impact they have on which groups, the actual effectiveness of the policy cannot be assessed.
