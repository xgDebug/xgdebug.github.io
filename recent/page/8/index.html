Without equity, there is no path to financial freedom.

If you do not hold equity in the company, the correlation between personal investment and income will be very strong. Almost all salaried jobs, even high-paying professions like lawyers or doctors, require an investment of time in order to earn the corresponding income. If you don’t hold equity in a business, you will have no income while sleeping, no income after retirement, and no income during vacations. In short, the income is relatively fixed and no substantial breakthrough can be achieved at all.
