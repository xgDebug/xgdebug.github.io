2023: The best way individuals believe to achieve happiness (or: The best way to obtain a sense of happiness in 2023)

Personally, I think the best way to achieve happiness is to transform life from a multiplayer online game into a single-player game. Online games like PUBG (Chicken Dinner) or Honor of Kings (King’s Land/Arena of Valor) require you to be better than others, have superior gear, and even compare skins. There are always stronger players among the strong, so disappointment is a highly probable event. But what if we change life into a single-player game? For example, Zelda—all you need to do is compare yourself to yourself. Every step is filled with a sense of happiness. Projecting this onto reality means having a better physique than others, a bigger house than others, and a fancier car than others. Constantly thinking about being superior to others, about having to surpass them, makes happiness difficult to achieve. In my opinion, the correct method is: buying a house so you don’t have to move around all the time, happy. Buying a car so you don’t have to squeeze onto the subway, happy.
