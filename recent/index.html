Without sales, even the best product is just a pile of expensive garbage. Underlying Logic: Are you still debating whether to write a perfect business plan? Stop writing it. Underlying Logic: Innovation means you have to bear 100% of the education market cost and the trial-and-error cost. Someone else has already spent millions validating that this demand is real; all you have to do is be 10% cheaper, or 20% better in service, or cover a traffic channel they haven’t reached yet. Conclusion: Put away your worthless “originality complex.” Find a competitor who is already making money, deconstruct them pixel by pixel, imitate them, and then surpass them. 3.
