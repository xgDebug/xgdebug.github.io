Conclusion: The Just Keep Buying Rules#

Highlight(yellow) - Page 281 · Location 4366 Saving is for the Poor, Investing is for the Rich Find where you are in your financial journey before deciding where to focus your time and energy. If your expected savings are greater than your expected investment income, focus on savings; otherwise focus on investing. If they are similar, focus on both. (Ch. 1) 翻译： 储蓄是为穷人准备的，投资是为富人准备的。在决定将时间和精力集中在哪里之前，先确定你在财务旅程中的位置。如果你的预期储蓄高于预期投资收入，就专注于储蓄；否则就专注于投资。如果两者相似，那就同时关注两者。(第 1 章)

Highlight(yellow) - Page 281 · Location 4373 Focus on Income, Not Spending Cutting spending has its limits, but growing your income doesn’t. Highlight(yellow) - Page 281 · Location 4376 Use The 2x Rule to Eliminate Spending Guilt If you ever feel guilty about splurging on yourself, invest the same amount of money into income-producing assets or donate to a good cause. This is the easiest way to have worry-free spending. (Ch. 4) 翻译： 使用“2倍规则”来消除消费内疚感。如果你因为为自己大手大脚消费而感到内疚，就把相同金额的钱投资到产生收入的资产中，或者捐赠给一个公益事业。这是实现无忧消费最简单的方法。(第 4 章)

Highlight(yellow) - Page 282 · Location 4379 Save at Least 50% of Your Future Raises and Bonuses A little lifestyle creep is okay, but keep it below 50% of your future raises if you want to stay on track. (Ch. 5) 翻译： 至少储蓄未来加薪和奖金的 50%。一点生活方式的膨胀是可以接受的，但如果你想保持正轨，请将其控制在未来加薪的 50% 以下。(第 5 章)

Highlight(yellow) - Page 282 · Location 4382 Debt Isn’t Good or Bad, It Depends on How You Use It Debt can be harmful in some scenarios and helpful in others. Use debt only when it can be most beneficial for your finances. (Ch. 6) 翻译： 债务并非好或坏，这取决于你如何使用它。债务在某些情况下可能有害，在其他情况下可能很有帮助。只有在它能对你的财务带来最大益处时，才使用债务。(第 6 章)

As a result, you should only do it when it fits into both your finances and your current lifestyle. (Ch. 翻译： 只有在时机成熟时才买房。买房很可能是你做过的最大财务决定。因此，你应该只在它符合你的财务状况和当前生活方式时才购买。(第 [章])

Highlight(yellow) - Page 282 · Location 4388 When Saving for a Big Purchase, Use Cash Though bonds and stocks may earn you more while you wait, when saving for a wedding, home, or other big purchase, cash is the way to go. (Ch. 8) 翻译： 为大额购买储蓄时，使用现金。尽管债券和股票在等待期间可能会为你赚取更多，但在为婚礼、房屋或其他大额购买储蓄时，现金是最佳选择。(第 8 章)

Highlight(yellow) - Page 283 · Location 4394 Invest to Replace Your Waning Human Capital with Financial Capital You won’t be able to work forever, so replace your human capital with financial capital before it’s too late. Highlight(yellow) - Page 283 · Location 4402 Buy Quickly, Sell Slowly Since most markets are expected to rise over time, buying quickly and selling slowly is the optimal way to maximize your wealth. (Ch. 13, 18) 翻译： 快速买入，缓慢卖出。由于大多数市场预计会随时间上涨，快速买入和缓慢卖出是最大化财富的最佳方式。如果你对这种方式感到不适，那么你正在买入/卖出的资产可能对你来说风险太高了。(第 13、18 章)

(Ch. 14) 翻译： 尽可能频繁地投资。如果你认为通过积累现金可以择时投资市场，那就再想想。即使是上帝也无法击败美元成本平均法（Dollar-Cost Averaging）。(第 14 章) Note - Page 283 · Location 4409 绝对不可能 翻译： 绝对不可能

Highlight(yellow) - Page 284 · Location 4412 Don’t Fear Volatility When It Inevitably Comes Markets won’t give you a free ride without some bumps along the way. Don’t forget that you have to experience some downside if you want to earn your upside. (Ch. 16) 翻译： 不要害怕波动性，因为它终将到来。市场不会在没有一些起伏的情况下给你免费的顺风车。不要忘记，如果你想获得上涨的收益，就必须经历一些下跌的波动。(第 16 章)

Highlight(yellow) - Page 284 · Location 4415 Market Crashes Are (Usually) Buying Opportunities Future returns are usually the highest following major crashes. Highlight(yellow) - Page 284 · Location 4418 Fund the Life You Need Before You Risk it for the Life You Want Though this book is called Just Keep Buying, sometimes it’s okay to sell.
